BatteryINFO – Smart BMS Monitor

BatteryINFO is an Android application designed to monitor and manage Bluetooth-enabled Battery Management Systems (BMS).

The app connects to compatible BMS devices using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), providing real-time battery information, monitoring, logging, and control features.

Key Features

- Live Battery Monitoring: View voltage, current, temperature, state of charge (SOC), and other available battery parameters.
- Automatic Bluetooth Connection: Automatically searches for and reconnects to your saved BMS device.
- BMS Pairing: Discover and connect to compatible Bluetooth BMS devices.
- Battery History: Review previously recorded battery information and device history.
- Live Data Logs: Monitor incoming BMS communication and battery updates.
- Battery Control: Access supported BMS control commands with password protection and confirmation.
- Multi-Pack Support: Discover and monitor multiple battery packs when supported by the BMS.

BatteryINFO provides a convenient interface for monitoring battery performance, tracking battery activity, and accessing supported BMS functions from your Android device.
